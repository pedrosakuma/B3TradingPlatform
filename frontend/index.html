    How to connect your bot
    
        
    
          A credential here authenticates a native FIXP/SBE order-entry
          connection — the same wire protocol B3 EntryPoint itself speaks, distinct
          from the REST/WebSocket API this console uses. Point your bot's FIXP client
          at the endpoint your operator configured
          (Trading:EntryPointListener:Endpoint), present this credential
          at logon, and it flows through the same pre-trade risk / persistence
          pipeline as orders placed here.
        
    
        
      
          
    • Protocol & design (RFC)
      • 
          
    • Connection & TLS setup guide
      • 
        
    
        
    
          This is a simulation. No real money, orders, or market
          access — fills and positions are synthetic. Credentials are personal and
          non-transferable; misuse leads to revocation.

    Consuming market data directly
    
        
    
          This app connects to the WS URL above as a plain, unauthenticated
          binary WebSocket — a separate service (B3MarketDataPlatform) from
          the trading-host's own /ws order/execution stream. A bot or
          algo can subscribe to the same endpoint directly instead of going through
          this UI.
