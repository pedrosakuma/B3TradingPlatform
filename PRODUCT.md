# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Professional traders and operational users working in a participant-side B3
order-management console. The primary audience mixes experienced electronic
traders with people who understand trading but are new to this platform and
need to learn its workflow without being slowed down.

## Product Purpose

B3TradingPlatform gives participant-side users a reliable interface for
monitoring market context, submitting and managing orders, and reviewing
executions, positions, balance, and risk controls. A successful first-run
experience gets a user to place a safe first order and recognize it in Working
Orders, while preserving immediate access for experts who already know what to
do.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and professional. The product should communicate operational
confidence, make risk legible, and stay out of the user's way under time
pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid forced SaaS tours, modal step carousels, decorative celebration,
patronizing explanations, and repeated popups. Do not turn the interface into a
consumer investing app or a tutorial mode disconnected from the real trading
workflow.

## Design Principles

- Lead users to real value, not feature education.
- Keep expert workflows immediately available and onboarding easy to skip.
- Explain risk and system state at the moment they affect an action.
- Prefer contextual, actionable guidance over blocking overlays.
- Preserve dense operational information while making the next safe action clear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. The full onboarding path must be keyboard operable, expose
clear focus states and semantic status updates, preserve meaning without color,
and respect reduced-motion preferences.
